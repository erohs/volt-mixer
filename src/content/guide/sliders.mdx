## Mouse and touch

| Do this                     | And                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Click anywhere on the track | The slider jumps there, and you can keep dragging.           |
| Tap the track with a finger | The slider jumps there. Swiping across it scrolls the page.  |
| Drag the handle             | It moves from where it is, as far as you drag.               |
| Double-click                | Back to rest: see the table below.                           |
| Turn the mouse wheel        | Small steps, once the slider has focus (click or Tab to it). |

Only the main mouse button moves a slider, and a slider only moves the way you drag: pressing and holding without moving never changes a level.

<Callout kind="caution" title="A press jumps straight there">
    A stray press high on the monitor or headphone level is a sudden loud jump. Drag the handle when you're not sure.
</Callout>

The mouse wheel only moves a slider you've clicked or tabbed to, so scrolling down the page never changes a level.

## Keyboard

Tab to a slider, then:

| Key                          | Faders and sends | Pan        | Gain    | Monitor, headphones |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------- | ------- | ------------------- |
| <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>↑</kbd> | +0.5 dB          | 1 right    | +0.5 dB | +0.5                |
| <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> | −0.5 dB          | 1 left     | −0.5 dB | −0.5                |
| <kbd>Page Up</kbd>           | +3 dB            | 10 right   | +3 dB   | +5                  |
| <kbd>Page Down</kbd>         | −3 dB            | 10 left    | −3 dB   | −5                  |
| <kbd>Home</kbd>              | Off (−∞)         | Full left  | 0 dB    | 0                   |
| <kbd>End</kbd>               | 0 dB             | Full right | Nothing | Nothing             |
| Double-click                 | 0 dB             | Centre     | 0 dB    | 0                   |

<Callout kind="tip" title="End never turns anything up to full">
    On a fader, End goes to 0 dB, wherever you start. On the monitor and headphone levels it does nothing.
</Callout>

Display brightness, in Settings, moves 1% with the arrows and 10% with Page Up and Page Down. Home turns the display off.

## How the faders move

- The top of a fader runs evenly from −40 to +12 dB, where you do most of your mixing. Below −40 it's squeezed down to off.
- It catches at **0 dB**: anything within about half a dB snaps to it.
- From off, the first step up lands on −60 dB.

## Getting around with a keyboard

- <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves through each strip in order: the gear, tone, 48V, fader, then mute, solo and link.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> presses a button.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes Settings or a channel's settings.
- Screen readers hear each slider's name and value, such as "Vocal mic level, minus 6.0 decibels".
