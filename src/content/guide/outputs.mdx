## The outputs panel

<Shot
    name="outputs"
    alt="The Outputs panel, with numbered parts: the Mix meter, Monitor's Mute and Mono buttons, the Monitor level and Headphones' Mute button."
/>

<Parts>

1. **Mix meter**
   The monitor mix, left and right, before the Monitor level.
2. **Mute (Monitor)**
   Silences your speakers.
3. **Mono**
   Folds the monitor mix to mono.
4. **Monitor level**
   Your speakers, from 0 to 100, the same scale as UAD Console.
5. **Mute (Headphones)**
   Silences the headphones. Their level is the slider below it, also 0 to 100.

</Parts>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Check your mix in mono">
    Press **Mono** for a moment while you listen. If something gets quieter or thinner, it may disappear on a phone
    speaker or in a mono stream.
</Callout>

## Cues

A cue is a mix for headphones. Each of the two can play:

- **Monitor mix**: the same as your speakers. The simple choice when it's just you.
- **Own mix**: its own level for each channel, from the [cue sends](../channel-settings/#cue-sends) in each channel's settings. For a performer who wants more of themselves, or less of the click.

The headphones play one cue, and Line Out 3/4 can play the other, so two people can each have their own mix. [A separate headphone mix](../use-cases/#a-separate-headphone-mix) walks through it.

<Callout kind="note">
    What the cues play is part of [your mix](../your-mix/), so each computer keeps its own, like the faders.
</Callout>
