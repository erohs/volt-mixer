## Record a podcast with two mics

_Uses: 48V, Auto-Gain, names and icons, meters._

1. Plug the mics into **Inputs 1 and 2**. For condenser mics, press **48V** on each strip and confirm.
2. Open the first mic's [settings](../channel-settings/) with its gear, choose **Auto-Gain › Start**, and have that person talk at their loudest until it finishes. Do the same for the second.
3. In each mic's settings, name it ("Host", "Guest") and pick the **Vocal mic** icon.
4. Leave the two unlinked, so each has its own fader and mute.
5. Watch the [meters](../channels/#meters) as you talk: green most of the time, amber on the loudest moments, never red.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Tip">
    If the clip light keeps coming on, take a few dB off that mic's gain in its settings.
</Callout>

## A separate headphone mix

_Uses: cues, cue sends, the outputs panel._

You listen on speakers; a singer in headphones wants more of their own voice.

1. Open **Settings › Cues and headphones**. Set **Headphones** to **Cue 1**, and **Cue 1** to **Own mix**.
2. Open each channel's settings and unfold **Cue 1**. Set its **Send**: their vocal up, the guitar down.
3. Set the headphone level in the outputs panel.

Your speakers still play the monitor mix, and your faders don't change their headphones: cue sends are taken before the fader.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Two people, two mixes">
    Set <strong>Cue 2</strong> to <strong>Own mix</strong> and give it its own sends, then set{" "}
    <strong>Line Out 3/4</strong> to <strong>Same as Cue 2</strong> and plug a headphone amp into Line Out 3/4.
</Callout>

## Play a games console through the Volt

_Uses: Line 3 and 4, link, names and icons, the tray._

1. Connect the console's left and right audio to **Line 3** and **Line 4** on the back.
2. Press **Link** on the Line 3 strip, so the console is one stereo strip.
3. In its settings, name it ("PS5") and pick the **Game console** icon.
4. Set its fader under your voice or music. −12 dB is a good start.
5. In the [desktop app](../desktop-app/), it's in the tray mixer, for a quick change mid-game.

## Bring an app's sound into the mix

_Uses: the Loopback strip._

Your computer's normal sound doesn't go through the faders. To mix an app in, such as music or a call:

1. In your computer's sound settings, send the app to the Volt's **LOOPBACK L/R** output. On Windows that's **Settings › System › Sound › Volume mixer**, then the app's output device.
2. Play something. The **Loopback** strip's meters move.
3. Set its fader, and rename it ("Music", "Calls").
4. **Mute** it to take it out of your speakers and headphones for a moment.

## Share one Volt between two computers

_Uses: mix links, the status line._

With the Volt on a KVM switch or moved by hand:

1. On the computer with the mix you want, open **Settings › Your mix** and copy the **Mix link**.
2. On the other computer, open the link. Volt Mixer uses it there.
3. From then on each computer sends its own copy when it connects. Bring the mix across again whenever you change it on one.

The status line says **Volt not connected · on the other computer?** while the Volt is away, and reconnects when it's back. More in [Other computers](../other-computers/).

## Take your mix to work on your phone

_Uses: the mix QR code, your phone's camera._

1. At home, open **Settings › Your mix** and choose **QR code**.
2. Scan it with your phone. Volt Mixer opens on the phone and keeps the mix.
3. At work, open Volt Mixer on the phone. It shows the mix as a QR code.
4. Scan it with the work computer's camera, or choose **Send the link** and open it there.

No app, no account and no Wi-Fi in common: the mix travels in the QR code. More in [Your phone](../your-phone/#carry-your-mix).

## Change levels from the sofa

_Uses: the desktop app, Phone remote._

1. In the desktop app, turn on **Settings › Desktop app › Phone remote**.
2. Scan the QR code with your phone, on the same Wi-Fi.
3. Ride the headphone, monitor and channel levels from your phone.

[Your phone](../your-phone/#phone-remote) has the details and what to try if it won't connect.
