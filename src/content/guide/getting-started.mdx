## Connect your Volt

1. Plug in your Volt, and close UAD Console if it's open on this computer.
2. Open **[Volt Mixer](https://naovixen.github.io/volt-mixer/)** in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
3. Choose **Connect Volt**. The browser lists your USB devices: pick **Volt 4 Gen 2**, then **Connect**.
4. Choose a starting mix (below).

<Callout kind="tip" title="Only once">
    The browser remembers your choice. From then on Volt Mixer connects by itself whenever the page is open and the Volt
    is plugged in.
</Callout>

## Choose a starting mix

The first time on each computer, Volt Mixer welcomes you and asks where your mix should start:

<Shot
    name="setup"
    alt="A welcome panel: “Welcome to Volt Mixer on this computer”, a Start a new mix button, and Bring my mix from another computer or phone underneath."
/>

- **New to Volt Mixer?** Choose **Start a new mix**. Every channel starts at 0 dB, and the faders unlock.
- **Already using it on another computer or your phone?** Open **Bring my mix** and paste its link. See [Other computers](../other-computers/).

The same panel comes back if you choose **Forget mix** in Settings. [Your mix](../your-mix/) explains why it asks.

## The status line

Beside the title, a short line says how the connection is going.

| It says                                     | It means                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Not connected                               | No Volt yet. Choose **Connect Volt**.                                                |
| Connected · firmware 1.0.3                  | Working, with the firmware the Volt reports.                                         |
| Disconnected                                | You chose **Disconnect**. The Volt keeps playing what it has.                        |
| Volt not connected · on the other computer? | The Volt went away, for example a KVM switch moved it. It reconnects when it's back. |

## Good to know

- **One Volt Mixer at a time.** If the page is open in two tabs, the second waits and takes over when the first closes. See [Other computers](../other-computers/#one-volt-mixer-at-a-time).
- **Close UAD Console.** It sends its own mix when it starts, over the top of yours.
- **Nothing to install**, but there's a [desktop app](../desktop-app/) if you'd like a mixer in the taskbar.

<Callout kind="caution" title="Firefox and Safari">
    They can't talk to USB devices, so Connect Volt stays greyed out. Open Volt Mixer in Edge or Chrome instead. On a
    phone, Volt Mixer can still [carry your mix](../your-phone/#carry-your-mix) to a computer.
</Callout>
