The main parts of the page:

<Parts>

1. **Connection status**
   Whether the Volt is connected, and its firmware.
2. **Connect Volt or Disconnect**
   Connects to the Volt, or lets it go.
3. **Settings**
   The Volt's own settings, what the headphones play, the view and your mix.
4. **Channels**
   One strip per input. Each has its level, tone and 48V, and buttons to mute, solo or link it. See [Channels](channels/).
5. **Outputs**
   How loud your speakers and headphones are. See [Outputs and cues](outputs/).

</Parts>

## What you need

- A Universal Audio **Volt 4 Gen 2**. Other Volts aren't supported yet.
- **Microsoft Edge** or **Google Chrome** on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS, or the [desktop app](desktop-app/). Firefox and Safari can't talk to USB devices.
- **UAD Console closed** on the same computer, since it sends its own mix when it starts.
