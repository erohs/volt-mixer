## Device

The Volt's own settings. The Volt remembers them, whichever computer it's on.

<Shot name="settings-device" alt="The Device and Cues and headphones groups in Settings." />

| Setting            | What it does                                                                 |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clip Protection    | The Volt's limiter, which catches sudden peaks before they distort.          |
| Display brightness | The Volt's display. 0% turns it off.                                         |
| Digital input      | Whether the optical port takes ADAT or S/PDIF. The digital strips follow it. |
| Find this Volt     | **Identify** flashes the Volt's lights, to tell which one is connected.      |

## Cues and headphones

What the headphones and the rear outputs play. See [Outputs and cues](../outputs/#cues) for how cues work.

| Setting      | Choices                                         | What it does                                      |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Headphones   | Cue 1, Cue 2                                    | Which cue you hear in the headphones.             |
| Cue 1, Cue 2 | Monitor mix, Own mix                            | The same as the speakers, or the cue's own sends. |
| Line Out 3/4 | Computer playback, Same as Cue 1, Same as Cue 2 | What the rear line outputs play.                  |

## View

How the page looks on this computer. None of these change what you hear.

<Shot name="settings-view" alt="The View group: Theme, Digital inputs and Loopback." />

| Setting        | What it does                                                            |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Theme          | Light, dark, or as your system. This guide follows it too.              |
| Digital inputs | Shows the S/PDIF or ADAT strips, whichever the digital input is set to. |
| Loopback       | Shows the Loopback strip. Hidden, it still plays at its level.          |

## Desktop app

Only in the [desktop app](../desktop-app/#settings): starting at sign-in, the tray, the Volt's display at sleep, the phone remote and updates.

## Your mix

<Shot
    name="settings-mix"
    alt="The Your mix group: the mix link with a copy button, a QR code toggle, a box to use a mix link, and the Apply and Forget buttons."
/>

| Setting        | What it does                                                                                       |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mix link       | Click it, or the copy button, to copy it. **QR code** shows it for a phone or another camera.      |
| Use a mix link | Paste a link from another computer or your phone. It replaces this computer's mix, and asks first. |
| Apply mix      | Sends this computer's mix to the Volt again.                                                       |
| Forget mix     | Removes this computer's mix. The Volt keeps playing what it has, and the welcome panel comes back. |

[Other computers](../other-computers/) shows these in use.

## About this Volt

The Volt's firmware and serial number, and the firmware Volt Mixer was checked against. The sample rate and clock aren't here: set them in your computer's sound settings or your audio app.
