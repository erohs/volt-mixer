## When the Volt moves

The Volt's own settings, like 48V, gain and the output levels, go with it. The faders don't: the Volt [can't report them](../your-mix/#who-remembers-what), so each computer sends the copy it last had when it connects.

- **The other computer has a mix:** it sends that one. To play your latest mix there, bring it across (below).
- **The other computer has never had one:** it leaves the Volt as it is, and asks you to choose a starting mix.

## Bring your mix across

### With a link

1. On the computer with the mix you want, open **Settings › Your mix** and click the **Mix link** to copy it.
2. Get it to the other computer, for example in an email to yourself, and open it there.
3. Volt Mixer uses it straight away, or keeps it until the Volt connects.

You can also paste a link into **Settings › Your mix › Use a mix link**, or into **Bring my mix** on the [welcome panel](../getting-started/#choose-a-starting-mix), and choose **Use**.

### With your phone

Scan **Settings › Your mix › QR code** with your phone. The phone keeps the mix, and shows it again as a QR code for the other computer's camera. Step by step in [Your phone](../your-phone/#carry-your-mix).

### With the other computer's camera

If the other computer is beside you, choose **QR code** in **Settings › Your mix** and scan it with that computer's camera.

<Callout kind="note" title="What's in a link">
    The whole mix, including your channel names, after the # in the address. Browsers don't send that part to any
    server, but anyone you give the link to can read it. Levels are rounded down very slightly, so a link can never make
    anything louder.
</Callout>

## When a link arrives

| If                                             | Then                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| This page is connected and in charge           | It's used straight away.                    |
| The Volt isn't connected yet                   | It's kept, and used when the Volt connects. |
| Volt Mixer is open in another tab              | It's sent to that tab, which uses it.       |
| The desktop app or another browser has the mix | It's used once that one closes.             |
| The browser can't reach a Volt (a phone)       | It's kept on the device, ready to carry.    |

## One Volt Mixer at a time

Two programs sending two different mixes would fight, so Volt Mixer takes turns on the same computer.

| Open at once                       | What happens                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Two tabs or windows of the website | The first is in charge. The second only shows the mix, and takes over when the first closes.                    |
| The website and the desktop app    | The app keeps the mix. The website leaves it alone until the app closes; levels, 48V and gain still work there. |
| Two browsers                       | Both leave the mix alone and say so, until one closes.                                                          |

Each case shows a note at the top of the page, so you know which one is in charge.
